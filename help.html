Using LuxVia

Practical guidance for preparing and sharing an order of service.

Choosing how to plan

You can choose a faith group or skip it. Ask Lux for guided planning, or make your own selections and return to Lux when you need help.

Sharing a PDF

In the booklet preview, choose Export PDF and then Share PDF (A5). This keeps the pages in reading order for someone opening the booklet on a phone, tablet or computer.

Printing a folded booklet

Choose Print-ready PDF (A4), or Print Booklet for the system print dialog. The print-ready version already has two A5 pages per side in booklet order. Use A4 landscape, double-sided printing with short-edge binding. Do not apply booklet or multiple-pages-per-sheet ordering again in the printer software.

Print one copy first. Fold the sheets and check that the covers are on the outside and the pages read in order. Some printers need different settings, so check the printer's preview and instructions.

Checking the date of death

The date of death can be filled in on the PDF. Check that the date is correct before sharing or printing. An entered date is included in the print-ready copy.

Reviewing writing from Lux

Read generated eulogies and poems as drafts. Check names, relationships and other details against what you know, and edit the wording before using it.
