Planning a service

There is no single way to remember a life. Begin with the choices that feel important, and let the rest take shape.

Choose your starting point

You can choose a faith group or continue without one. A service template can give you a starting order; you can also make your own selections. If the service follows a particular tradition, speak to the person conducting it about what is appropriate.

Bring together words and music

Think about the readings, songs and memories that reflect the person. Include your own words where they matter. Keep the practical details close at hand: where the ceremony will take place, who will speak and the time available.

Ask for help when you want it

Lux can guide you with one short question at a time. You can also explore the app yourself and return to Lux when a question comes up.

Review the service together

Check the names, dates, running order and wording with the family and the person conducting the ceremony. Leave enough time for readings, music and transitions.

Prepare the booklet

Open the booklet preview and check every page before exporting. Use the normal PDF for reading on a device, or the print-ready version when you want a folded booklet.
