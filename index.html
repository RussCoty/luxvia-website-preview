Practical questions

Before you begin.

Do I need to choose a religion?

No. A faith group is optional. You can continue without choosing one and build the service around your own preferences.

Do I have to use Lux?

No. Choose guided help if it is useful, or navigate the app yourself. Lux is there when you want assistance.

Can I change a service template?

A template provides a starting order, not a fixed ceremony. Review the selections and wording with the person conducting the service.

Can I print an order of service?

Yes. The booklet preview offers a PDF for reading and a print-ready A4 version for folding. Print the A4 version double-sided with short-edge binding, without applying booklet ordering a second time in the printer software.

Can Lux help write a eulogy or poem?

Lux can help draft a tribute from the details and memories you provide. Review the draft, check each factual detail and edit it before including it in a service.
