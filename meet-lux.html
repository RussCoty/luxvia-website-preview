Meet Lux

Lux is the memorial planning assistant inside LuxVia. It is there to help you find a next step, not to make the choices for you.

Guidance at your pace

Ask for guided planning and take one short question at a time. If you prefer to choose items yourself, Lux remains available for help as you go.

Your memories come first

The details you provide are the starting point for a tribute. Stories, everyday habits, important relationships and favourite memories can help give a eulogy or poem a personal voice.

A draft to make your own

AI-generated writing should be treated as a draft. Read it carefully, check the facts and change anything that does not sound like you. Only include it in the service when you are happy with it.
