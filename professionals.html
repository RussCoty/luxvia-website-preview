LuxVia Pro

Care for each family. Keep each service in view.

LuxVia Pro brings client details, funeral cases, dates and preparation notes into a professional workspace for funeral directors, celebrants, clergy and officiants.

Organise clients and services

Keep separate professional client workspaces and funeral cases. Record the family contact, the person being remembered, the venue and the service date. Keep notes on wishes and details still to be agreed with each case.

See what needs attention

See upcoming services and cases that need review. Use the calendar and case status to keep preparation in view, from the first conversation to the final family review.

Make room for the family's wishes

Use the family's selections to discuss the tone of the service, the readings and music, and who will contribute. A prepared order of service can make it easier to see what has been chosen and what still needs a decision.

Agree the practical details

Check timing, venue requirements and the expectations of the relevant faith community. Confirm the final order and wording with the family before printing or sharing the booklet.

Review a clear booklet

A PDF lets everyone review the same order of service. Use the reading-order version for discussion and proofing, then prepare the print version when the content is agreed.
